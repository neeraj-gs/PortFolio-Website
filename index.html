Building Intelligent Systems That Think, Act & Scale

Currently leading product development at Penomo, where I'm building CredOS, an AI-powered operating system for infrastructure private credit. I architect multi-agent pipelines, design LLM-driven automation flows, and own the complete product lifecycle from customer discovery through deployment.
